# Henry's preferences

Plain-English rules learned from Henry's feedback. The daily run reads this
before scoring and adds to it at most once a week. Edit or delete any rule.

## From the calibration set (20 Oct 2026)

- Thermal management must be central. Power electronics, motors and EV
  news without a thermal angle are not interesting.
- Heat-exchanger innovation outside the three areas still counts (for
  example refinery antifouling technology, fusion and microreactors).
- Barriers to adoption matter less at this stage.
- The Conflux article on what data centres can learn from F1 is the best
  example so far: a real cooling challenge, plus AM, system modelling and CFD.
- One-off repair or sourcing stories are never interesting.
